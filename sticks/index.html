01 / Principle

A proposer may suggest.
Only the verifier commits.

An analyst or a language model may propose a procedure, but a step enters the plan only when its bindings, preconditions, execution domain and evidence obligations hold. A step with no admissible candidate is refused rather than filled in, and every value the reconstruction supplies keeps its provenance.

02 / Method

One finite domain.
Every commitment recorded.

  1. IntelligencePinned ATT&CK Enterprise 19.2 bundle.
  2. CatalogVersioned procedure templates with declared bindings.
  3. AdmissionDeterministic checks against a scoped campaign state.
  4. ExecutionCertified by evidence predicates, not exit status.
  5. ProvenanceEach supplied value recorded as an explicit commitment.

03 / Evidence state

Proposers diverge

Without a catalog, six independent reconstructions commit 5.9 distinct implementations per step, with 0.5% pairwise agreement.

The catalog converges

With the catalog, pairwise agreement reaches 88.5%. The verifier keeps any proposal that fails admission out of the plan.

Reach is bounded

In a three-campaign slice, 25 of 25 steps are admitted once contracts are reconstructed and none from the raw bundle. The trusted catalog supports 252 of 1,146 steps (22.0%) in 55 campaigns with linked techniques, out of 56 active campaigns.
