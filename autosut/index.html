01 / Principle

Intelligence narrows the environment.
It does not determine it.

AutoSUT separates what structured fields support, what a declared rule derives and what an analyst commits, and reports each against an explicit denominator. A commitment never becomes evidence without the record showing it.

02 / Method

One measurement contract.
Every number traceable.

  1. CorporaATT&CK Enterprise, Mobile and ICS, CAPEC, and FiGHT.
  2. HistorySeven Enterprise releases, pinned by SHA-256.
  3. PolicyDeclared routing rules, ablated under four fallback policies.
  4. ComparisonTwo blinded LLM judges from different model families, with disagreement retained.
  5. ConsequenceControlled two-arm execution study.

03 / Evidence state

Version evidence is flat

Across seven releases, software objects with a version signal stay between 2.1% and 2.8%, and none carries a CPE identifier.

Routing labels depend on policy

Model-judge consensus places 33.0% of techniques in the container-feasible class, against 2.7% under the conservative policy. Neither rate establishes execution feasibility.

Choices change runs

In one controlled surrogate study, two SUTs that satisfy the same measured CTI constraints execute 10 and 1 of ten steps of the same procedure.
